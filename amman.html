Amman, Jordan

Discover the historic landmarks of Jordan's capital and the beautiful green landscapes of nearby Ajloun.

Top Attractions in Jordan

Amman Citadel

A historic hilltop archaeological site offering ancient ruins and panoramic views of Amman.

Roman Theater

An impressive ancient amphitheater in downtown Amman, famous for its Roman architecture.

Ajloun Forest

A scenic natural area in northern Jordan with green hills, forests, and peaceful walking trails.

Travel Information

Country: Jordan

Best Time to Visit: Spring and autumn

Activities: Historical tours, photography, and nature trips
