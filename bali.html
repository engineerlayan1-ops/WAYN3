Bali, Indonesia

Discover beautiful beaches, ancient temples, green rice terraces, and unforgettable tropical adventures in Bali.

Top Attractions in Bali

Uluwatu Temple

A famous sea temple on a dramatic cliff overlooking the Indian Ocean, especially beautiful at sunset.

Tegallalang Rice Terrace

Explore stunning green rice terraces near Ubud and enjoy Bali's peaceful countryside.

Nusa Penida

A beautiful island famous for Kelingking Beach, dramatic cliffs, and clear turquoise water.

Travel Information

Country: Indonesia

Best Time to Visit: April to October

Activities: Sightseeing, beaches, photography, and nature walks
